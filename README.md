# noisetta

I build Linux desktop tools for people who care about how their audio actually sounds.

Tinkerer. Audiophile. KDE Plasma on CachyOS. Python when it needs to work, QML when it needs to look right.

---

## Projects

**[Auris](https://github.com/noisetta/auris)** — audio library scanner for Linux. Detects fake lossless files, clipping, dynamic range issues, and spectral content. Tells you what you actually have in your collection, not just what the filename says.

**[Audio HUD](https://github.com/noisetta/audio-hud)** — native KDE Plasma 6 widget that shows live PipeWire audio stats on your desktop: codec, sample rate, bit depth, and bit-perfect status. For when you want to know exactly what's coming out of your DAC.

**[HiBy Mods](https://github.com/hiby-modding/hiby-mods)** — custom firmware modifications for HiBy digital audio players, along with documentation of the proprietary OTA firmware format. Started as a solo project, now a community effort under the [hiby-modding](https://github.com/hiby-modding) organisation.
